B. Area threshold (small regions vs. WCAG's 25%-of-screen rule)

NeuroShield's own unit tests show a single 8×8 "region" (1/16 = 6.25% of frame) triggers detection on its own. WCAG's actual area threshold is 25% of any 10° field of view. If these trip the overlay, NeuroShield is more conservative than WCAG requires — worth documenting as a false-positive risk, not just a miss.

C. Multi-frame ramp (sub-threshold adjacent-frame deltas)

Targets detectors that only diff adjacent frames rather than tracking a transition across its full duration.
