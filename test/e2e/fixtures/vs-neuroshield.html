FlashGuard vs. NeuroShield

Set FlashGuard's sensitivity to Low before this page (popup → sensitivity slider, all the way left) — the literal WCAG-standard threshold, not our own unvalidated "Balanced"/"High" presets.

Real ffmpeg-rendered clips from neuroshield-files/, each confirmed live against a real NeuroShield install at every one of its sensitivity settings. With both extensions enabled: FlashGuard intervenes on the left video and leaves the right one alone; NeuroShield does the opposite on both.
